# Simulation Input Audit

## Main Assumptions
- The POIs are 61 across 29 neighbourhoods.
- Tourist segments include architecture_fans, history_religion, food_local_markets, family_green, beach_nightlife, and museum_culture.
- Rules are calibrated from popularity concentration and central hotspot pressure with a Gini coefficient of 0.2293 and top 10 central hotspots accounting for 80% of the overall pressure.

## Acceptability for Seminar Experiment
The inputs are acceptable for a seminar experiment as they provide a structured framework to simulate tourist behavior without relying on real-world data, which can be noisy or unavailable. The curated POIs and tourist segments ensure that the simulation is focused and relevant to the seminar's objectives. The rule calibration metadata indicates that the rules are based on statistical analysis of popularity concentration and central hotspot pressure, making them suitable for guiding the simulation.

## Main Limitations
- **Limited Scope**: The inputs do not represent real-world conditions but are tailored for a specific seminar scenario.
- **Simplified Segmentation**: While tourist segments cover various interests, they may not fully capture the diverse preferences of actual tourists.
- **Calibration Data**: The Gini coefficient and central hotspot share indicate moderate concentration in popularity, which might not reflect extreme variations seen in real-world scenarios.

Overall, these inputs provide a coherent and focused simulation environment for the seminar experiment.
