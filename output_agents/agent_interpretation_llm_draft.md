# Interpretation of Simulation Metrics

- **Satisfaction**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.6774) vs Personalized (0.7036) strategies offer higher satisfaction levels compared to the Popularity strategy (0.5970). The trade-off here is that the sustainable and personalized approaches may not cover as many POIs, potentially leading to less diverse experiences.

- **Peak Crowding Ratio**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.3067) has a lower peak crowding ratio compared to Personalized (0.3200) and Popularity (0.5133). This suggests that the sustainable approach manages visitor density more effectively, reducing overcrowding issues.

- **Neighbourhood Entropy**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.9707) has a higher neighbourhood entropy compared to Personalized (0.8020) and Popularity (0.3652). This indicates that the sustainable approach may distribute visitors more evenly across different areas, potentially leading to less concentrated tourist flows.

- **Top-5 POI Visit Share**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.2516) has a lower top-5 POI visit share compared to Personalized (0.3532) and Popularity (0.6478). This suggests that the sustainable approach may not cover as many popular POIs, which could limit visitor satisfaction.

- **Central Visit Share**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.2734) has a lower central visit share compared to Personalized (0.4022) and Popularity (0.9072). This indicates that the sustainable approach may not attract as many visitors to central areas, which could affect local economies.

- **Hotspot Visit Share**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.1500) has a lower hotspot visit share compared to Personalized (0.2566) and Popularity (0.9068). This suggests that the sustainable approach may not attract as many visitors to popular hotspots, potentially impacting local economies.

- **POI Coverage**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.8772) has a higher POI coverage compared to Personalized (0.8070) and Popularity (0.2807). This indicates that the sustainable approach covers more diverse areas, which could lead to better local economies but may result in less concentrated visitor experiences.

- **Precision@5**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.7099) has a lower precision at five compared to Personalized (0.9759) and Popularity (0.3125). This suggests that the sustainable approach might not accurately predict popular POIs, leading to less efficient visitor distribution.

- **Local Economy Score**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.8217) has a higher local economy score compared to Personalized (0.7427) and Popularity (0.8727). This indicates that the sustainable approach may have better economic impacts, but it might not cover as many popular POIs.

- **Green Visit Share**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.2844) has a lower green visit share compared to Personalized (0.1818) and Popularity (0.0570). This suggests that the sustainable approach may attract fewer visitors who prioritize environmental sustainability, potentially impacting local economies.

- **Average Distance**: 
  - Sustainable Tourism (0.8716 km) has a lower average distance compared to Personalized (0.8882 km) and Popularity (0.3426 km). This indicates that the sustainable approach may keep visitors closer to their starting points, potentially reducing travel costs but limiting exploration of diverse areas.

In summary, while Sustainable Tourism offers higher satisfaction levels and better local economy scores, it also has lower coverage of popular POIs and hotspots, as well as a more even distribution of visits. Personalized and Popularity strategies provide different trade-offs in terms of visitor density, economic impact, and environmental sustainability.
